# Lembar Cepat (Cheat Sheet) Ringkasan Trigonometri

Halaman ini berisi rangkuman rumus-rumus inti dan konsep kunci dari seluruh rangkaian materi trigonometri untuk mempermudah proses *review* cepat sebelum ujian.

---

## 1. Segitiga Siku-Siku & Sudut Istimewa

### Rasio Dasar Sisi Siku-Siku
- **$\sin \theta = \frac{de}{mi}$** (Depan/Miring)
- **$\cos \theta = \frac{sa}{mi}$** (Samping/Miring)
- **$\tan \theta = \frac{de}{sa}$** (Depan/Samping)

### Nilai Kunci Kuadran I
- $\sin 0^{\circ}=0 \quad | \quad \sin 30^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2} \quad | \quad \sin 45^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2} \quad | \quad \sin 60^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3} \quad | \quad \sin 90^{\circ}=1$
- $\cos 0^{\circ}=1 \quad | \quad \cos 30^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3} \quad | \quad \cos 45^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2} \quad | \quad \cos 60^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2} \quad | \quad \cos 90^{\circ}=0$
- $\tan 0^{\circ}=0 \quad | \quad \tan 30^{\circ}=\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{3} \quad | \quad \tan 45^{\circ}=1 \quad | \quad \tan 60^{\circ}=\sqrt{3} \quad | \quad \tan 90^{\circ}=\infty$

---

## 2. Aturan Tanda Kuadran & Sudut Berelasi
- **Kuadran I** ($0^{\circ} - 90^{\circ}$): **Semua** fungsi (+).
- **Kuadran II** ($90^{\circ} - 180^{\circ}$): Hanya **Sinus** (+). Rumus horizontal: $180^{\circ} - \alpha$
- **Kuadran III** ($180^{\circ} - 270^{\circ}$): Hanya **Tangen** (+). Rumus horizontal: $180^{\circ} + \alpha$
- **Kuadran IV** ($270^{\circ} - 360^{\circ}$): Hanya **Cosinus** (+). Rumus horizontal: $360^{\circ} - \alpha$

---

## 3. Identitas Utama & Persamaan Dasar
- **Identitas Pythagoras:** $\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1$
- **Solusi Sinus:** $x = \alpha + k \cdot 360^{\circ}$ atau $x = (180^{\circ} - \alpha) + k \cdot 360^{\circ}$
- **Solusi Cosinus:** $x = \pm\alpha + k \cdot 360^{\circ}$
- **Solusi Tangen:** $x = \alpha + k \cdot 180^{\circ}$

---

## 4. Rumus Segitiga Sembarang

### Aturan Sinus
$$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$$

### Aturan Cosinus
$$a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc \cdot \cos A$$

### Luas Trigonometri
$$\text{Luas} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot a \cdot b \cdot \sin C$$

---

## 5. Rumus Analitis Lanjut

### Jumlah / Selisih Sudut
- $\sin(A \pm B) = \sin A \cos B \pm \cos A \sin B$
- $\cos(A \pm B) = \cos A \cos B \mp \sin A \sin B$

### Sudut Ganda
- $\sin 2A = 2 \sin A \cos A$
- $\cos 2A = \cos^2 A - \sin^2 A = 2\cos^2 A - 1 = 1 - 2\sin^2 A$

### Penjumlahan ke Perkalian Fungsi
- $\sin P + \sin Q = 2 \sin\left(\frac{P+Q}{2}\right) \cos\left(\frac{P-Q}{2}\right)$
- $\cos P + \cos Q = 2 \cos\left(\frac{P+Q}{2}\right) \cos\left(\frac{P-Q}{2}\right)$
- $\cos P - \cos Q = -2 \sin\left(\frac{P+Q}{2}\right) \sin\left(\frac{P-Q}{2}\right)$
